Answer every question. No penalty for wrong answers.

Sections run in order. Start the Section 1 timer; when it ends, or when you press Next section, the Section 2 timer begins. In practice you may still scroll back, but treat Section 1 as closed once Section 2 starts.

A calculator is allowed for all of Math. Grid-ins take one number; negatives and fractions are fine.

How a real test differs: each real module is longer (27 R&W, 22 Math) and Module 1 performance sets the difficulty of an adaptive Module 2. This mock has no adaptive stage. Nothing is scored until you press Submit. Progress is saved in this browser.
