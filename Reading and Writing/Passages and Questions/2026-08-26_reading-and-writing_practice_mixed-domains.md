Which choice completes the text with the most logical and precise word or phrase?

- **A)** austere
- **B)** vibrant
- **C)** chaotic
- **D)** derivative

**2.**

Roman concrete structures such as the Pantheon have survived two millennia, while modern concrete often degrades within decades. Researchers long attributed this durability to the volcanic ash in the Roman mix. A 2023 study, however, identified the small white chunks of lime in Roman samples — previously dismissed as evidence of careless mixing — as a self-healing mechanism: when water enters a crack, it dissolves the lime, which then recrystallizes and seals the gap.

Which choice best states the main idea of the text?

- **A)** Roman concrete outlasts modern concrete because of the volcanic ash it contains.
- **B)** A feature of Roman concrete once considered a defect turns out to explain its durability.
- **C)** Researchers in 2023 determined that the Pantheon contains chunks of lime.
- **D)** Modern concrete would last longer if it were mixed less carefully.

**3.**

A researcher measured the germination rate of a wildflower species at four soil temperatures:

| Soil temperature | Germination rate |
|---|---|
| 10 °C | 22% |
| 15 °C | 54% |
| 20 °C | 81% |
| 25 °C | 38% |

The researcher concluded that germination does not rise steadily with temperature but instead peaks and then declines.

Which choice most effectively uses data from the table to support the researcher's conclusion?

- **A)** At 10 °C, only 22% of seeds germinated — the lowest rate of any temperature tested.
- **B)** Germination rose from 22% at 10 °C to 81% at 20 °C, then fell to 38% at 25 °C.
- **C)** Germination at 15 °C (54%) was higher than germination at 25 °C (38%).
- **D)** The highest germination rate recorded was 81%, which occurred at 20 °C.

**7.**

While researching a topic, a student has taken the following notes:

- The Silk Road was a network of trade routes, not a single road.
- It connected China to the Mediterranean.
- It operated from roughly the 2nd century BCE to the 15th century CE.
- Goods traded along it included silk, spices, paper, and gunpowder.
- Ideas and religions, including Buddhism, also travelled along it.

The student wants to emphasize the Silk Road's role in transmitting things that were not material goods. Which choice most effectively uses relevant information from the notes to accomplish this goal?

- **A)** The Silk Road, a network of trade routes rather than a single road, connected China to the Mediterranean.
- **B)** Operating from about the 2nd century BCE to the 15th century CE, the Silk Road carried silk, spices, paper, and gunpowder.
- **C)** Along with goods such as silk and spices, the Silk Road carried ideas and religions — Buddhism among them — from China to the Mediterranean.
- **D)** The Silk Road was in use for more than a thousand years, from roughly the 2nd century BCE to the 15th century CE.
